## What makes this track distinct

**Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat (Basic)** is the **free, universal on-ramp**: AI chat grounded on the **web**, wrapped in **enterprise data protection**, included with eligible Microsoft 365 business licences at no extra cost. Its defining limit — and the thing every learner must internalize — is that it **cannot see your organization's work data**. No mail, no files, no chats, no meetings. It can run pay-as-you-go agents, upload a file you hand it in-session, and generate images, but grounding stops at the web.

> **Distinction to hold:** Basic answers general questions safely; the moment you need it to reason over *your* documents or mail, you need [**Premium**](../copilot-chat-premium/).
