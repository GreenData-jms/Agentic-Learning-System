# Track 7 — Copilot in Word  · *(Scaffold)*

**Family:** Microsoft 365 · **Surface:** Word (requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence) · **Grounded in:** the active document + Graph · **Rank:** Mate

## What makes this track distinct

Copilot in Word helps **draft** content (with or without formatting), **rewrite** and adjust tone, and **summarize or answer questions** about the document — and it can ground a draft in other files you reference. Its distinctive discipline is **voice and accuracy ownership**: the human remains the author, and a generated draft is a starting point to shape, fact-check, and make your own. Requires a **Premium** ([Microsoft 365 Copilot](../copilot-chat-premium/)) licence.

> **Distinction to hold:** the deliverable is prose *you will put your name on*. That raises the bar on voice, accuracy, and citation beyond any code-completion task.
