# Track 9 — Copilot in Teams  · *(Scaffold)*

**Family:** Microsoft 365 · **Surface:** Teams meetings & chat (requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence) · **Grounded in:** meeting transcript & chat history + Graph · **Rank:** Mate

## What makes this track distinct

Copilot in Teams works over **conversations**: it produces **meeting recaps** and real-time meeting notes, **catches you up** on chat threads (with recent history), and captures **key points and action items** from calls. Its distinctive discipline is **conversational accuracy and etiquette** — recaps can miss nuance or misattribute, and there are norms and consent considerations around meeting AI. Requires a **Premium** ([Microsoft 365 Copilot](../copilot-chat-premium/)) licence.

> **Distinction to hold:** the raw material here is *what people said*. A recap is an interpretation to verify before you act on it — especially for decisions and owners.
