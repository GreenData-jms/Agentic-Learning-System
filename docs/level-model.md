# Level Model — The Mate Rank, Three Bands, Three Threads

This document defines the shared learning frame every track uses. Learn it once; it applies everywhere.

---

## The rank: Mate

The Agentic Learning System's entry rank is **Mate**. A Mate is a **capable, safe, productive operator** of a given Copilot — not a specialist, not a platform architect, not an administrator. The whole of this curriculum lives inside the Mate rank. Ranks above Mate (specialist, architect) are out of scope here.

Inside Mate there are three **bands**. They are progression checkpoints, reached by demonstrated competence rather than time served.

| Band | Identity | The learner can… | Done when they can… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mate · Beginner** | First contact | Set up the tool, produce a first useful result, and articulate what the tool is *not* for. | Ship one real, verified output and name one thing they would *not* trust Copilot to do unchecked. |
| **Mate · Intermediate** | Daily driver | Use the tool fluently in real work, shape context deliberately, and apply core best practices unprompted. | Complete a normal day's task predominantly through the tool, correctly choosing modes and providing context. |
| **Mate · Advanced** | Power user | Configure and customize the tool, build and automate multi-step workflows, and mentor a Beginner. | Stand up a reusable, customized workflow others can adopt, and coach someone through Beginner. |

---

## The three threads

Every track teaches to the same spine. The threads are **parallel**, not sequential — a learner develops all three at once, and each deepens as they move through the bands.

### Mindset — *how to think*
The judgment layer. When to reach for Copilot and when not to; staying the author and the reviewer; calibrating trust to the task's stakes; the verification reflex; instincts for data sensitivity, security, and licensing. Mindset is what keeps a fast tool from becoming a fast way to be wrong.

### Toolset — *what to use*
The surface layer. The actual modes, panels, settings, files, and switches of the specific Copilot. For GitHub Copilot that means completions vs. chat vs. edit vs. agent mode, custom instructions, and MCP. For Copilot in Excel it means the Copilot pane, supported data shapes, and what it can and cannot compute. Toolset is knowing the instrument.

### Skillset — *what to do, and automate*
The technique layer. Repeatable patterns: how to prompt, how to supply context, how to decompose a task, and — at the Advanced band — how to turn a repeated task into a configured, semi-automated workflow. Skillset is where fluency becomes leverage.

## The 3 × 3 matrix

Every track is, at heart, this grid. The track's chapters fill each cell.

| | **Mindset** | **Toolset** | **Skillset** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beginner** | What it is / isn't; author-in-the-loop | Install, sign in, the primary surface | First useful result; verify it |
| **Intermediate** | Trust calibration; when *not* to use it | Modes & context controls; settings | Prompting patterns; context-shaping |
| **Advanced** | Governance, data, security instincts | Customization & configuration | Building & automating workflows; mentoring |

---

## How progression works

1. A learner enters a track at **Beginner** regardless of seniority elsewhere — the tool is new even if the domain is not.
2. They work all three threads together; the track's labs are tagged by band.
3. They advance when they can **demonstrate** the band's "done when" criteria, checked against the track's competency checklist.
4. **Advanced is not mastery of the platform** — it is mastery of the *Mate* scope: fluent, safe, self-sufficient, and able to bring one other person up to Beginner.
